Privacy Policy for Bubble Shooter: Pop & Match

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app Bubble Shooter: Pop & Match (package com.hertz.bubbleshooter, "the app"), published by Hertz Studio ("we", "us"), handles information. If you have questions, contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com.

Summary

  • The app has no accounts, no sign-up and no login.
  • Your progress, items and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run any servers and we do not receive this data.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob, limited to content rated G (suitable for all ages).
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices.
  • The app is not directed at children under 13.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device, using Android's local app storage:

  • Game progress: the levels you reached and the stars for each, your Arcade best and top-10 scores, and game statistics such as bubbles popped
  • Coins, boosters (bombs, rainbows, fireballs, aim guides and extra moves), collected cards and bubble skins
  • Achievements (trophies) and your daily reward streak
  • Settings: sound effects, vibration and colorblind mode

This data never leaves your device through the app and is not sent to us. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this data in your device's backup to your Google account, or copy it to a new device during setup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us. You can delete all of this data at any time by clearing the app's storage (Settings > Apps > Bubble Shooter > Storage > Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free to play and is supported by ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). The app shows:

  • Banner ads at the bottom of menu screens such as the level map and the shop (never during gameplay)
  • Occasional full-screen ads at natural breaks between levels, at most every few levels
  • Optional rewarded video ads that you can choose to watch for in-game rewards, such as free coins, extra shots, an Arcade continue or doubled level coins

Every ad request asks for a maximum ad content rating of G. To show, limit, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • IP address, used to estimate an approximate (city or country level) location, never precise location
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version and language
  • Ad interactions, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as SDK crash logs and ad loading performance

Ads may be personalized based on this information where you have given consent or where the law allows it; otherwise Google shows non-personalized ads. This data is processed by Google under its own policies, not by us. We do not receive information that identifies you personally, and we do not sell any data. Data sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Google uses this information as described in its policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google advertising technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

3. Consent and your choices

  • Consent form: the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where required by law, such as in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states, it shows a consent message before personalized ads are requested.
  • Ad privacy choices: where this applies to you, Settings > Ad privacy choices in the app lets you review or change your consent at any time.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device).
  • Rewarded video ads are always optional and clearly labelled.

4. Internet and permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only to load and show ads and to check consent status with Google.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID, added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK): used only for advertising, as described above.

The app does not use precise location, and it does not ask for the location, camera, microphone, contacts, notifications or storage permissions. The levels are created on your device, so the game can be played without an internet connection (ads are not shown while offline).

5. Data we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store, sell or receive any personal information from the app. We do not use our own analytics or crash-reporting services. Data collected by Google AdMob is handled by Google as described above.

6. Data security

Information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is sent to Google using encrypted connections (HTTPS). Data stored on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox.

7. Children

The app is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to content rated G. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the app, contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com and we will help you, including with requests about ad-related data processed by Google.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to or restrict its use. Because we do not hold any of your data, you can exercise these rights over the data on your device by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it, and over advertising data by using the ad privacy choices described above, by contacting Google, or by asking us for help. You may also contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com.
