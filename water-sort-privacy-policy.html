Privacy Policy

Water Sort Puzzle by Hertz Studio · Effective 25 November 2026

The short version

  • Water Sort Puzzle is a colour-sorting puzzle game for children and families.
  • We do not ask for names, emails, photos, location or any other personal information.
  • There are no accounts, chats or ways to talk to other players.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • There are no ads and no in-app purchases.
  • The game works offline. The only thing it sends is a short technical crash report (with no personal information) if the game crashes, so we can fix the problem.

1. Who we are

Water Sort Puzzle ("the app") is made by Hertz Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why. If you are a child, please read it with a parent or guardian.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, store or share personal information. The app does not ask for, and we do not receive:

  • your name, email address, phone number or home address;
  • photos, videos, voice recordings or contacts;
  • precise or approximate location;
  • the device's advertising ID (the app is built so that it cannot read it).

We do not run our own servers and do not use analytics or tracking tools. The only information that leaves the device is a technical crash report if the game crashes (see section 5).

3. Information saved on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • levels completed and stars earned;
  • in-game coins, liquid and tube looks, backgrounds, achievements and statistics such as levels won (these have no real-money value);
  • your daily challenge streak and daily reward days;
  • settings such as sound, vibration, theme, colour-blind symbols and the daily reminder;
  • the puzzle you are playing, so it can be resumed.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may copy this game data to your Google account backup so it can be restored on a new device. This is managed by Google under your device settings, not by us. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your device settings, deletes the data from the device.

4. Advertising

The current version of Water Sort Puzzle shows no ads. The advertising code in the app is switched off, so no ad is ever requested, Google's consent form is never shown, and Google AdMob receives no information from the game. There are no reward videos.

If we switch ads on in a future update, we will update this policy before that update is released. Any ads would come from Google AdMob, which is certified for apps for children under Google Play's Families policy, and every ad request would be marked as child-directed (COPPA) and under the age of consent (EU and UK GDPR), limited to family-safe (G-rated) content, never personalised, and would not use the advertising ID.

5. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

To find and fix bugs, Water Sort Puzzle uses Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google. If the game crashes or hits a serious error, it sends a crash report. A crash report contains only technical information:

  • what went wrong in the game's code (the stack trace), the game version and basic game state, such as the level being played;
  • the device model, Android version and similar technical details (memory, screen orientation);
  • a random identifier created for crash reporting, used only to count how many devices are affected. It is not the advertising ID and is not linked to you.

Crash reports never include your name, email, contacts, location, photos or anything you type, and nothing in them identifies a child. They are used only to fix problems, not for advertising, and are not sold or shared. Google processes them on our behalf and keeps them for 90 days, then deletes them. You can read more at firebase.google.com/support/privacy. To ask for crash data to be deleted sooner, email us at the address below.

6. Notifications and permissions

  • Notifications (optional): after a few wins the game asks once whether you would like a daily reminder. If you say yes and Android allows it, the app shows one notification in the evening when the daily puzzle hasn't been played. The reminder is scheduled on the device; nothing is sent to us. You can turn it off in Settings at any time.
  • Internet and network state: used only to send crash reports (section 5). It is also declared for advertising, which is switched off in this version.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

The app does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, storage or location.

7. In-app purchases and links

Water Sort Puzzle has no in-app purchases. Coins are earned by playing and cannot be bought with real money.

There are no links that leave the app. This privacy policy and the terms are stored inside the app, and links in them are switched off.

8. Children's privacy

We designed Water Sort Puzzle so that children can play without sharing personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Crash reports contain only technical information and nothing that identifies a child. If you are a parent or guardian and believe information about your child has reached us, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Game data on the device can be erased at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

10. Security

Game data stays in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. We do not transmit it anywhere. Crash reports are sent to Google over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and include the new version in an app update. If a change would mean collecting personal information from children, we would first obtain verifiable parental consent where the law requires it.
