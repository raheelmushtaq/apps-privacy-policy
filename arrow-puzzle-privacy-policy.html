Privacy Policy for Arrow Flow

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app Arrow Flow – Logic Puzzle (package com.hertz.arrowpuzzle, "the app"), published by Hertz Studio ("we", "us"), handles information. If you have questions, contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com.

Summary

  • The app has no accounts, no sign-up and no login.
  • Your progress, achievements and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run any servers and we do not receive this data.
  • Arrow Flow is designed for players of all ages, including children under 13. We treat every player as a child.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. All ads are non-personalized and rated G (suitable for all ages). The app does not collect the advertising ID, and no interest-based or remarketing ads are shown.
  • Because no personalized advertising takes place, the app does not show an ad consent form.
  • The optional daily reminder is a notification created on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device, using Android's local app storage:

  • Game progress: the levels you reached and the best stars for each, Hard mode clears, daily challenges completed (for streaks and monthly trophies), streak freezes, weekly event boards solved, and your Survival best score
  • Achievements and collection progress, such as perfect clears and letter boards solved
  • Hints you own
  • Settings: play mode (Relaxed, Normal or Hard), sound, vibration, grid, arrow colour theme, daily reminder on/off, and whether you have seen the tutorial

This data never leaves your device through the app and is not sent to us. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this data in your device's backup to your Google account, or copy it to a new device during setup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us. You can delete all of this data at any time by clearing the app's storage (Settings > Apps > Arrow Flow > Storage > Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free to play and is supported by ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). The app shows banner ads, occasional full-screen ads between levels, and optional rewarded video ads that you can choose to watch for in-game rewards such as hints, undoing a mistake, a second chance, extra time in Survival or a streak freeze.

Google AdMob is certified for use in apps for children under Google Play's Families policy. Every ad request from the app is marked as child-directed (for COPPA) and under the age of consent (for the GDPR and UK GDPR), with a maximum ad content rating of G. This means, for every player:

  • Ads are not personalized, and no interest-based advertising or remarketing is used.
  • The Android advertising ID is not collected (the app removes the advertising ID permission).
  • Only ads suitable for general audiences (rated G) are requested.

To show these contextual ads, limit how often ads appear, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may still process limited information such as:

  • IP address, used to estimate an approximate (city or country level) location, never precise location
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version and language
  • Ad interactions, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as SDK crash logs and ad loading performance

This data is processed by Google under its own policies and under child-directed treatment, not by us. We do not receive information that identifies you personally, and we do not sell any data. Data sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Google uses this information as described in its policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google advertising technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

3. Consent and your choices

  • No consent form: the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) only to tell Google that the user is under the age of consent. As a result, no consent form is shown and personalized ads are never requested, wherever you live.
  • Ad privacy choices: the Settings > Ad privacy choices entry only appears if Google's consent tool reports that it is required, which is not expected for this setup.
  • Advertising ID: the app does not read your advertising ID, so resetting it in your device settings has no effect on this app.
  • Rewarded video ads are always optional and clearly labelled.

4. Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder (in Settings, or when the app offers it after your first daily challenge), the app schedules a notification on your device for the evening, shown only if you have not played that day's challenge. On Android 13 and later, Android asks for your permission first. The reminder is created entirely on your device, and nothing is sent to us. You can turn it off at any time in the app's Settings or in Android's notification settings.

5. Internet and permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only to load and show ads and to check the consent status with Google (no consent message is shown).
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): used only for the optional daily reminder.

The app removes the advertising ID permission that the Google Mobile Ads SDK would otherwise add. It does not use precise location, and it does not ask for the location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions. The puzzles are created on your device, so the game can be played without an internet connection.

6. Data we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store, sell or receive any personal information from the app. We do not use our own analytics or crash-reporting services. Data collected by Google AdMob is handled by Google as described above.

7. Data security

Information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is sent to Google using encrypted connections (HTTPS). Data stored on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox.

8. Children

Arrow Flow is designed for players of all ages, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We follow Google Play's Families policy and treat every player as a child, whatever their age:

  • We do not ask for or collect any personal information. There is no account, chat, user-generated content or in-app purchase.
  • Every ad request is tagged as child-directed under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and as under the age of consent under the GDPR and UK GDPR (including the rules for children, sometimes called "GDPR-K"). Google therefore shows only non-personalized, G-rated ads.
  • The app does not collect the Android advertising ID, and no interest-based advertising or remarketing is used.
  • No ad consent form is shown, because no personalized advertising or consent-based processing takes place.
  • Rewarded videos are optional and clearly labelled; full-screen ads appear only at natural breaks between levels.
  • The daily reminder is off until it is turned on, and Android asks for permission before any notification is shown.

Parents and guardians can contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com with any question, or to ask us to delete data. We (the developer) hold no personal data about your child: game progress stays on the device and can be deleted by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app, and we will help you with any request about ad-related data processed by Google.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to or restrict its use. Because we do not hold any of your data, you can exercise these rights over the data on your device by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it, and over advertising data (processed by Google under child-directed treatment) by contacting Google or asking us for help. You may also contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com.
