Privacy Policy — Notes

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Android app Notes (package com.hertz.notes, "the app") handles information. The app is published by Hertz Studio ("we", "us"). If you have questions, contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com.

Summary

  • You do not need an account. There is no sign-up and no login.
  • Your notes, checklists and photos are stored only on your device. We do not have servers that receive them and we cannot read them.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect certain device information to show and measure ads, as described below and subject to your consent where the law requires it.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally, in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Notes and checklists you create (titles, text, checklist items, color, pinned state, created and edited times), kept in an on-device database.
  • Photos you attach to notes. When you take a photo or pick one from your gallery, a copy is saved inside the app's private storage so the note keeps its picture. Images that are no longer attached to any note are cleaned up automatically.
  • App settings (theme, dynamic color and sort order).

This information is not sent to us or to any third party by the app. It stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.

Android backup. The app allows Android's built-in backup. If you have device backup turned on in your Android settings, your notes, attached photos and settings may be included in your device backup, which is handled by Google under your Google account and Google's own privacy terms. You can turn device backup on or off in your Android system settings. We do not have access to these backups.

Permissions. The app requests internet access (needed to load ads). It does not request permission to access your camera, contacts, location, or your device's shared storage. When you add a photo, the app uses the system camera app or the system photo picker, and only the photo you choose is shared with the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve, personalize (where permitted), measure and prevent fraud in ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • Device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app-set ID;
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general (approximate) location;
  • App interaction and ad data, such as app launches, ad views and taps;
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as crash logs and SDK performance data;
  • General device information, such as device model, operating system version and language.

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Data is transmitted using encryption (HTTPS). For details, see:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • AdMob data disclosure: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/11233480

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states)

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent where required by law (for example, under the GDPR in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, or under applicable US state privacy laws). Ads are only requested after the consent process has completed. Depending on your choices, Google may show non-personalized ads, which do not use your past behavior for targeting but still use some data for frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention.

Where this applies, you can review or change your choice at any time from Ad privacy settings in the app's menu.

Your advertising controls

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, under Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device). You can also manage Google ad settings at https://adssettings.google.com.

3. Sharing and feedback

  • Sharing a note. When you tap Share, the note's text (and its photo, if any) is passed to the app you choose through Android's share sheet. What happens next is governed by that app.
  • Sending feedback. If you choose "Send feedback", your email app opens a pre-filled message to us that includes the app version, your device manufacturer and model, and your Android version, to help us fix problems. You can edit or remove this before sending. If you send it, we receive your email address and the message content and use them only to reply to you and improve the app.
  • Links. "Rate the app", "More apps" and "Privacy policy" open Google Play or a web page. Those services have their own privacy policies.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not require or create user accounts.
  • We do not collect your notes, checklists or photos.
  • We do not use any analytics or crash-reporting service of our own.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Notes and photos: delete any note inside the app at any time. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes all notes, photos and settings from your device.
  • Device backups: managed by you in Android and Google account settings.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google according to its policies. You can reset your advertising ID and manage ad settings as described above.
  • Feedback emails: you can ask us to delete your correspondence by writing to raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com.

6. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not ask for your name, age, email address or other personal details. If you believe a child has provided us personal information (for example, by email), contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com and we will delete it.

7. Security

Your notes are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access on a standard Android device. Communication between the ads SDK and Google uses encryption in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please protect your device with a screen lock.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, or to object to its processing. Because we do not hold your notes or any account data, most requests concern advertising data processed by Google; you can exercise those through the in-app Ad privacy settings, your device's ad settings, or Google directly. For anything else, contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com.

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, with the effective date shown above.
