Privacy Policy

Hop Aboard by Hertz Studio · Effective date: 13 November 2026

The short version (for kids)

  • We never ask for your name, email, photo, location or anything else about you.
  • There are no accounts, no chat and no way to talk to strangers in the game.
  • Your levels, stars and coins are saved only on this device.
  • There are no ads in the game.
  • If the game crashes, a crash report helps us fix it. It says what went wrong in the game, never who you are.
  • If you are not sure about something, ask a parent or grown-up.

1. Who we are

Hop Aboard ("the game") is made by Hertz Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is handled when you play the game, and why. The game is designed for children, and we treat every player as a child, whatever their age.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. We do not ask for, and the game cannot record, your name, email address, phone number, photos, voice, contacts or precise location. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no social features and no user-generated content.

Game data kept on your device

Your progress (levels, stars and moves), coins, boosters, unlocked vehicle and parking lot styles, and settings (sound, vibration, language) are stored only on your device. We never receive them. If your device's Android backup is turned on, Android may include this game data in the backup kept by Google for your device account. You can control this in your device settings.

3. Advertising

The current version of the game shows no ads. The advertising code in the app is switched off, so no ad is ever requested and Google AdMob receives no information from the game. If ads are switched on in a future update, we will update this policy before that update is released, and the ads will work as described in the rest of this section.

If ads are switched on, the game shows them using Google AdMob, which is certified for use in apps for children under Google Play's Families policy. Every ad request from the game is set up for children:

  • Marked as child-directed (under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA) and as coming from a user under the age of consent (for the EEA, the UK and Switzerland).
  • No personalised or interest-based ads and no remarketing. Ads are chosen from the content of the game, not from information about the player.
  • Only content rated "G" (suitable for general audiences) is allowed.
  • The game does not use the device advertising ID; the permission for it is removed from the app.

To deliver ads, limit how often the same ad is shown, prevent fraud and count ad views, Google may process limited technical information such as the IP address (which may be used to estimate the country or region), device type, operating system version, app information and whether an ad was shown or tapped. Google handles this information under its own policies, with the restrictions that apply to child-directed requests. You can read how Google uses information in apps that show ads at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google's advertising policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Optional reward videos

If ads are switched on, some features let a player choose to watch a short video ad for a reward, for example coins, an extra parking bay for one round, or a vehicle or parking lot style. Watching is always optional, the game can be played without it, and nothing is ever bought with real money.

Consent in some countries

In some regions, such as the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent tool may be shown before ads load. If it is, the choices can be changed at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices. Ads remain non-personalised whatever is chosen.

4. Crash reports

To find and fix bugs, the game uses Firebase Crashlytics, a service from Google. If the game crashes or hits a serious error, a crash report is sent. It contains technical information only: what went wrong in the game's code, the level being played, the game version, the device model, the Android version, and a random installation identifier created for crash reporting (not the advertising ID, and not linked to any person). Crash reports never include names, contact details, photos, location or anything typed by the player. Google processes this information on our behalf to provide the service, and we use it only to improve the game's stability. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days and then deleted. You can read more at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

6. App permissions

  • Internet and network state: to send crash reports (and to load ads, if they are switched on in a future update), and to check for a connection before trying.
  • Vibration: for short game feedback, which can be turned off in Settings.

7. Sharing

We do not share, sell or rent any information about players. The only third party involved is Google, which processes crash reports as described in section 4 (and would show ads, as described in section 3, if they are switched on in a future update).

8. Keeping and deleting data

We hold no player data on servers. Crash reports, which contain no personal information, are deleted automatically after 90 days. To delete the game data on your device, use Settings → Progress → Reset all progress, or uninstall the game. For information held by Google about ads, see Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

9. Children's privacy and parents' rights

We follow the principles of COPPA (USA), the UK GDPR and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, and the EU General Data Protection Regulation as they apply to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from any child. Parents and guardians can contact us at any time with questions, or to ask us to review or delete information. If you believe a child's information has reached us by mistake, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Security

Game data stays in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Crash reports are sent over secure connections.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it in the game and change the effective date above. If a change affects how information is handled, we will make it clear in the game before it applies.
