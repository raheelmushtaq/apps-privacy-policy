Privacy Policy for Word Roo

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app Word Roo (package com.hertz.wordroo, "the app"), published by Hertz Studio ("we", "us"), handles information. If you have any questions, contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com.

Summary

  • Word Roo does not require an account, sign-up or login.
  • We (the developer) do not collect, store or receive your personal information on any server of our own.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • The app is intended for players aged 13 and over.
  • Your use of the app is also covered by our Terms and Conditions.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only, so your progress is kept between sessions:

  • Game progress: puzzles played and won, streaks, scores, achievements, your in-game coin balance, the outfits you have unlocked or bought with coins, which puzzles you have recently seen or solved, and your Daily Challenge results.
  • Settings: sound effects and vibration on or off, the number of chances per puzzle, which animal you play as, what it is wearing, and whether you have seen the how-to-play screen.

We do not receive any of this data. Games in progress are not saved: leaving a game ends it. You can delete your data at any time with Settings ▸ Reset progress, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app. If Android backup is turned on, your progress and settings may be included in your device's own Google backup, which is controlled by you and Google, not by us.

All puzzles are built into the app and work without an internet connection.

Advertising

The app is free and supported by ads provided by Google AdMob:

  • a banner ad on the home screen (never while you are guessing a word);
  • an occasional full-screen ad between puzzles, at most once every 3 minutes and never while you are guessing;
  • optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch, to reveal a letter or to carry on a Play run with a new word. You never have to watch one.

Ads are limited to content rated suitable for general audiences (G). We can't guarantee that every ad will suit every user, but you can report an inappropriate ad from the ad itself.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as the advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), basic device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings ▸ Ad privacy choices (shown when it applies to you). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings.

Google handles this information under its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • AdMob and data: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

In-game coins

Coins are a play-only currency earned by solving puzzles. They cannot be bought with real money and have no cash value. There are no in-app purchases.

Children

Word Roo is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no sign-in, chat, or sharing between users. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and you have concerns, contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and Google's consent status.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

The app does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

Data security

The app has no servers of its own. Data that Google's ad services collect is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
