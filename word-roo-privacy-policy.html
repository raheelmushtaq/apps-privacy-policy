Privacy Policy for Word Roo

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app Word Roo (package com.hertz.wordroo, "the app"), published by Hertz Studio ("we", "us"), handles information. If you have any questions, contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com.

Summary

  • Word Roo does not require an account, sign-up or login.
  • We (the developer) do not collect, store or receive your personal information on any server of our own.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Word Roo is designed for players of all ages, including children under 13. We treat every player as a child.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. All ads are non-personalised and rated G (suitable for all ages). The app does not use the advertising ID, and no interest-based or remarketing ads are shown.
  • Because no personalised advertising takes place, the app does not show an ad consent form.
  • Your use of the app is also covered by our Terms and Conditions.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only, so your progress is kept between sessions:

  • Game progress: puzzles played and won, streaks, scores, achievements, your in-game coin balance, the outfits you have unlocked or bought with coins, which puzzles you have recently seen or solved, and your Daily Challenge results.
  • Settings: sound effects and vibration on or off, the number of chances per puzzle, which animal you play as, what it is wearing, and whether you have seen the how-to-play screen.

We do not receive any of this data. Games in progress are not saved: leaving a game ends it. You can delete your data at any time with Settings ▸ Reset progress, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app. If Android backup is turned on, your progress and settings may be included in your device's own Google backup, which is controlled by you and Google, not by us.

All puzzles are built into the app and work without an internet connection.

2. Advertising

The app is free and supported by ads provided by Google AdMob, a Google Play Families self-certified ads SDK:

  • a banner ad on the home screen (never while you are guessing a word);
  • an occasional full-screen ad between puzzles, at most once every 3 minutes and never while you are guessing;
  • optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch, to reveal a letter or to carry on a Play run with a new word. Each button says it plays an ad, and you never have to watch one.

Because Word Roo is designed for children as well as adults, every ad request is marked as child-directed and under the age of consent, with personalised ads turned off and the maximum ad content rating set to G. This means:

  • ads are contextual, based on the app itself, not on the player's interests or behaviour;
  • no interest-based advertising, remarketing or cross-app tracking takes place;
  • the app does not collect or use the Android advertising ID (the permission is removed from the app).

To deliver these ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may still process limited technical information, such as the device's IP address (which can indicate approximate location), basic device and app information, and how ads are viewed or tapped. Google uses this only as allowed for child-directed requests. We do not receive this information. We can't guarantee that every ad shown will suit every user, but you can report an inappropriate ad from the ad itself or by emailing us.

Google handles this information under its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • AdMob and data: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

3. In-game coins

Coins are a play-only currency earned by solving puzzles. They cannot be bought with real money and have no cash value. There are no in-app purchases.

4. Children's privacy

Word Roo is designed for players of all ages, including children under 13, and complies with Google Play's Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children (or from anyone else):

  • there is no sign-in, chat, social features, or sharing between users;
  • the app does not ask for a name, email address, photo, location or any other personal information;
  • ads are child-directed, non-personalised and rated G, as described above;
  • links that leave the app (for example to Google's privacy pages) are behind a simple grown-up check.

Parents and guardians can delete all of the app's data at any time with Settings ▸ Reset progress, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app. If you are a parent or guardian and have any questions or concerns, contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com and we will respond promptly.

5. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

The app does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files, and it does not use the advertising ID.

6. Data security

The app has no servers of its own. Data that Google's ad services process is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
