Privacy Policy for Sudoku

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app Sudoku (package com.hertz.sudoku, "the app"), published by Hertz Studio ("we", "us"), handles information. If you have questions, contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com.

Summary

  • The app has no accounts, no sign-up and no login.
  • Your games, settings and statistics are stored only on your device. We do not run any servers and we do not receive this data.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect certain device information to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the UK, EEA and Switzerland, and in some US states), the app asks for your consent using Google's consent tool before ads use your data, and you can change your choice at any time.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device, using Android's local app storage:

  • Game progress: in-progress puzzles (one per game mode), unlocked levels and stars, Beat the Clock best score, puzzles solved, and completed daily challenges
  • Statistics: games started and won, best and average times for each difficulty
  • Hints and lives you own, and the date you last opened the daily reward chest
  • Settings: music, sound effects and vibration on/off

This data never leaves your device through the app and is not sent to us. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this data in your device's backup to your Google account, or copy it to a new device during setup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us. You can delete all of this data at any time by clearing the app's storage (Settings > Apps > Sudoku > Storage > Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free to play and is supported by ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). The app shows banner ads, occasional full-screen ads between games, and optional rewarded video ads that you can choose to watch for in-game rewards such as a hint, a life, a second chance or extra time.

To deliver, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share with Google:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general (approximate) location
  • Ad interactions, such as ads viewed and tapped
  • Diagnostic information, such as app and SDK performance and crash data related to ads
  • Basic device information, such as device model, operating system version and language

Google uses this information as described in its policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google advertising technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

The app does not use precise location, and it does not ask for the location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permissions.

3. Consent and your choices

  • Consent: When required by law, the app shows a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform when it starts. Ads are only requested once the consent requirements allow it. Depending on your choice, you may see personalized or non-personalized ads.
  • Changing your choice: If the consent requirement applies to you, open Settings > Ad privacy choices in the app at any time to review or change your choice.
  • Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (for example Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device).

4. Internet and permissions

The app uses the internet and network-state permissions only to load and show ads and the consent message. The puzzles themselves are created on your device, so the game can be played without an internet connection.

5. Data we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store, sell or receive any personal information from the app. We do not use our own analytics or crash-reporting services. Data collected by Google AdMob is handled by Google as described above.

6. Data security

Information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is sent to Google using encrypted connections (HTTPS). Data stored on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox.

7. Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com and we will help.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to or restrict its use. Because we do not hold any of your data, you can exercise these rights over the data on your device by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it, and over advertising data through the consent options above, your device's ad settings, or by contacting Google. You may also contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com.
