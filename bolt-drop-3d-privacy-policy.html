Privacy Policy

Bolt Drop 3D by Hertz Studio · Effective 18 November 2026 · Last updated 25 November 2026

The short version

  • Bolt Drop 3D is a family-friendly 3D screw-sort puzzle game designed for players of all ages, including children.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases. We do not ask for or collect your name, email, photos, contacts or location.
  • Your progress, items and settings are saved only on your device. We do not run any servers and we never receive this data.
  • The app may show ads from Google AdMob. All ad requests are strictly configured for child-directed treatment (COPPA / TFCD) with a General Audiences content rating (G). No personalised ads, interest-based tracking, or Advertising ID (AD_ID) collection is performed.
  • Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), consent is gathered via Google UMP with child/under-age-of-consent protections enabled.
  • Your use of the app is also covered by our Terms and Conditions.
  • If the game crashes, it sends a short technical crash report (with no personal information) to Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the problem.

1. Who we are

Bolt Drop 3D ("the app"), package com.hertz.boltdrop3d, is made by Hertz Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, who handles it and the choices you have.

2. Information saved on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • game progress: unlocked campaign levels, the stars earned on each, Daily Challenge and weekend Golden Rush progress, and your best score in Beat the Clock;
  • in-game coins, boosters and cosmetics you own or have equipped (screw skins, toolbox themes, backgrounds and effects). These have no real-money value;
  • achievements, game statistics (such as levels completed and screws removed), your daily login reward streak, and how many free-coin rewards you have used today;
  • a level you are in the middle of, so it can be resumed;
  • which tips you have already seen, and your settings for sound effects and vibration.

The app never sends this data to us, except that a crash report (section 4) may include basic game state such as the level being played. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include it in your device's backup to your Google account, or copy it to a new device during setup, so your progress can be restored. That backup is handled by Google under your device and Google account settings, not by us.

You can delete all of this data at any time by clearing the app's storage (Settings → Apps → Bolt Drop 3D → Storage → Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Bolt Drop 3D is free to play. The app may show family-friendly ads provided by Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK):

  • a banner ad on some menu screens;
  • occasional full-screen ads between levels, never in the middle of a puzzle;
  • optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch, for example for free coins, a booster, unlocking a storage slot or doubling a level reward. A reward is given only if the ad is watched to the end.

Ad requests from Bolt Drop 3D are explicitly configured with child-directed treatment (COPPA / TFCD) and a General Audiences content rating (G). The app completely removes the device Advertising ID (AD_ID) permission. Personalised ads and interest-based ad tracking are strictly disabled. Google Mobile Ads SDK serves only non-personalised, contextual ads suitable for general and child audiences.

Contextual ad processing by Google is handled under Google's policies, encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Learn more:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google advertising technologies: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

4. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

To find and fix bugs, Bolt Drop 3D uses Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google. If the game crashes or hits a serious error, it sends a crash report. A crash report contains only technical information:

  • what went wrong in the game's code (the stack trace), the game version and basic game state, such as the level being played;
  • the device model, Android version and similar technical details (memory, screen orientation);
  • a random identifier created for crash reporting, used only to count how many devices are affected. It is not the advertising ID and is not linked to you.

Crash reports never include your name, email, contacts, location, photos or anything you type, and nothing in them identifies a child. They are used only to fix problems, not for advertising, and are not sold or shared. Google processes them on our behalf and keeps them for 90 days, then deletes them. You can read more at firebase.google.com/support/privacy. To ask for crash data to be deleted sooner, email us at the address below.

5. Consent and your choices

  • Consent form: the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) with under-age-of-consent tags enabled so that consent requests respect child privacy laws where applicable (e.g. EEA, UK, Switzerland).
  • Privacy options: where Google's consent tool reports that options are required, a Privacy options button appears in Settings.
  • Rewarded videos are always optional and clearly offered as ads.

6. Information we do not collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store or receive personal information from the app. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or location. There are no accounts, chats or ways to contact other players.

We do not run our own servers, and the app does not use any analytics service. The only crash-reporting service is Firebase Crashlytics (section 4).

7. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used to load and show contextual ads, to check consent status with Google and to send crash reports (section 4). The puzzles themselves can be played offline.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

The app does not request Advertising ID (AD_ID), camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or location permissions.

8. In-app purchases

Bolt Drop 3D has no in-app purchases. Coins, boosters and cosmetics are earned by playing or by choosing to watch rewarded ads, and cannot be bought with real money.

9. Children's privacy

Bolt Drop 3D is a family-friendly game designed for players of all ages, including children under 13. We strictly comply with child privacy regulations, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and Google Play Families Policy.

We do not collect, request, store, or share any personal information from children or any other users. All game progress and settings remain solely on your device, and crash reports contain only technical information and nothing that identifies a child.

Advertising in the app is strictly configured with child-directed treatment (TFCD) and General Audiences (G) content rating, and Advertising ID tracking is disabled. If you have any questions or requests regarding your child's privacy, you can contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we do not hold your data, you can erase all game data on your device at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

11. Security

Game data stays in the app's private storage on your device, protected by Android's app sandbox so other apps cannot read it. Information sent to Google's ad and crash-reporting services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).
