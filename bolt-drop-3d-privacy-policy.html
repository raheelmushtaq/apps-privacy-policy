Privacy Policy

Bolt Drop 3D by Hertz Studio · Effective 18 November 2026 · Last updated 18 November 2026

The short version

  • Bolt Drop 3D is a 3D screw-sort puzzle game for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases. We do not ask for your name, email, photos, contacts or location.
  • Your progress, items and settings are saved only on your device. We do not run any servers and we never receive this data.
  • The app may show ads from Google AdMob. When ads are shown, Google may use your device's advertising ID and other device data, and ads may be personalised where your consent choices allow it.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google's consent form asks for your choice first, and you can change it later from Settings → Privacy options.
  • Your use of the app is also covered by our Terms and Conditions.

1. Who we are

Bolt Drop 3D ("the app"), package com.hertz.boltdrop3d, is made by Hertz Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, who handles it and the choices you have.

2. Information saved on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • game progress: unlocked campaign levels, the stars earned on each, Daily Challenge and weekend Golden Rush progress, and your best score in Beat the Clock;
  • in-game coins, boosters and cosmetics you own or have equipped (screw skins, toolbox themes, backgrounds and effects). These have no real-money value;
  • achievements, game statistics (such as levels completed and screws removed), your daily login reward streak, and how many free-coin rewards you have used today;
  • a level you are in the middle of, so it can be resumed;
  • which tips you have already seen, and your settings for sound effects and vibration.

The app never sends this data to us. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include it in your device's backup to your Google account, or copy it to a new device during setup, so your progress can be restored. That backup is handled by Google under your device and Google account settings, not by us.

You can delete all of this data at any time by clearing the app's storage (Settings → Apps → Bolt Drop 3D → Storage → Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Bolt Drop 3D is free to play. The app may show ads provided by Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK):

  • a banner ad on some menu screens;
  • occasional full-screen ads between levels, never in the middle of a puzzle;
  • optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch, for example for free coins, a booster, unlocking a storage slot or doubling a level reward. A reward is given only if the ad is watched to the end.

When ads are shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID (the app does not remove the advertising ID permission that the SDK uses);
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate an approximate (city or country level) location, never precise GPS location;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version and language;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped;
  • diagnostic information, such as ad loading performance and SDK errors.

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and, where your consent choices allow it, to show personalised ads based on your interests. Ad requests from Bolt Drop 3D are not marked as child-directed. Where personalised ads are not allowed, Google may still show non-personalised ads that rely on limited, contextual information.

This information is collected and used by Google under its own policies, not by us. We do not receive information that identifies you, and we do not sell any data. Data sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Learn more:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google advertising technologies: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Tapping an ad may open a web page or another app, such as Google Play. Those are run by the advertiser or by Google under their own privacy policies.

4. Consent and your choices

  • Consent form: the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where the law requires consent before ads use personal data (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google's consent form is shown when you open the app, and ads are only requested in line with your choice. If no consent is required where you are, no form is shown.
  • Privacy options: where Google's consent tool reports that it is required, a Privacy options button appears in the app's Settings so you can review or change your choice at any time.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (on most devices: Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads). You can also manage Google ad personalisation at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Rewarded videos are always optional and clearly offered as ads.

5. Information we do not collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store or receive personal information from the app. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or location. There are no accounts, chats or ways to contact other players.

We do not run our own servers, and the app does not use any analytics or crash-reporting service. The only third party that receives data from the app is Google, through AdMob and its consent tool, as described above.

6. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used to load and show ads and to check your consent status with Google. The puzzles themselves can be played without a connection.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or location.

7. In-app purchases

Bolt Drop 3D has no in-app purchases. Coins, boosters and cosmetics are earned by playing or by choosing to watch rewarded ads, and cannot be bought with real money.

8. Children's privacy

Bolt Drop 3D is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Because the game is not designed for children, ad requests are not marked as child-directed and ads may be personalised where consent allows.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 is using the app, you can remove all game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it, and you can contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com. We hold no data about your child ourselves, but we will help with any request about data processed by Google.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its use, or to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold your data, you can:

  • erase all game data on your device by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app;
  • change your ad consent choice from Settings → Privacy options (where shown) and manage your advertising ID in your device settings;
  • exercise your rights over advertising data with Google, or ask us for help at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com.

10. Security

Game data stays in the app's private storage on your device, protected by Android's app sandbox so other apps cannot read it. Information sent to Google's ad services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

We may update this policy, for example when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top and included in an app update.
