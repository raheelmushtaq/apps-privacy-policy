Privacy Policy

Math Quest by Hertz Studio · Effective 25 November 2026 · Last updated 25 November 2026

The short version

  • Math Quest is a family-friendly arithmetic puzzle game for players of all ages, including children.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no in-app purchases. We do not ask for or collect your name, email, photos, contacts or location.
  • Your progress, items and settings are saved only on your device. We do not run any servers and we never receive this data.
  • If the app crashes, an anonymous crash report is sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the problem. It contains technical details only, never your progress or anything you type.
  • The app does not use the Advertising ID and has no analytics or tracking.
  • Your use of the app is also covered by our Terms and Conditions.

1. Who we are

Math Quest ("the app"), package com.hertz.mathquest, is made by Hertz Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, who handles it and the choices you have.

2. Information saved on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • game progress: unlocked worlds and levels, the stars earned on each, Daily Challenge results and your daily streak, and your Endless Mode runs and best score;
  • in-game coins and gems, and the tile themes and avatars you own or use. These have no real-money value;
  • achievements and game statistics (such as puzzles solved and time played);
  • a puzzle you are in the middle of, so it can be resumed, including Endless puzzles generated on your device;
  • whether you have seen the welcome screen, and your settings for sound effects, vibration, animations and reduced motion.

The app never sends this data to us. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include it in your device's backup to your Google account, or copy it to a new device during setup, so your progress can be restored. That backup is handled by Google under your device and Google account settings, not by us.

You can delete all of this data at any time by clearing the app's storage (Settings → Apps → Math Quest → Storage → Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app.

3. Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes, it sends a crash report to Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can find and fix the problem. A report contains the error details (stack trace), the app version, the device model, Android version, available memory and disk space, the state of the app at the time (for example screen orientation) and random installation identifiers that count how many installs are affected. These identifiers do not reveal who you are and are used only to keep the app working, never for advertising or profiling.

Crash reports do not include your name, contact details, location, game progress or anything you type. Google keeps crash data for 90 days. It is encrypted in transit and processed by Google on our behalf. Learn more:

  • Firebase privacy and security: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

4. Information we do not collect

We, the developer, do not collect or store personal information from the app. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or location. There are no accounts, chats or ways to contact other players. There are no ads, and the app uses no analytics or advertising service.

5. Permissions

  • Internet: used only to send crash reports. Every puzzle, including the Daily Challenge and Endless Mode, plays offline.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

The app does not request the Advertising ID (AD_ID), camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or location.

6. No ads and no in-app purchases

Math Quest shows no ads and has no in-app purchases. Coins, gems and cosmetics are earned by playing and cannot be bought with real money.

7. Children's privacy

Math Quest is designed for players of all ages, including children under 13. We follow child privacy rules, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR's rules for children and the Google Play Families Policy.

We do not collect, request, store or share personal information from children or any other players. Game progress and settings stay on the device. The only information that leaves the device is the anonymous crash report described in section 3, which is used solely to support the internal operation of the app (finding and fixing crashes).

If you have questions about your child's privacy, contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because we do not hold your data, you can erase all game data on your device at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. For questions about crash reports, contact us.

9. Security

Game data stays in the app's private storage on your device, protected by Android's app sandbox so other apps cannot read it. Crash reports are sent to Google over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top and included in an app update.
