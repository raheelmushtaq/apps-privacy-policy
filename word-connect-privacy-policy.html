Privacy Policy

Word Connect by Hertz Studio · Effective 19 November 2026

The short version

  • Word Connect is a word puzzle game for everyone, including children.
  • We do not ask for names, emails, photos, location or any other personal information.
  • There are no accounts, chats or ways to talk to other players.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • There are no ads in the game.
  • The game does not go online. It works fully offline.
  • There are no in-app purchases.

1. Who we are

Word Connect ("the app") is made by Hertz Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles and why. If you are a child, please read it with a parent or guardian.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not collect, store or share personal information. The app does not ask for, and we do not receive:

  • your name, email address, phone number or home address;
  • photos, videos, voice recordings or contacts;
  • precise location;
  • the device's advertising ID (the app is built so that it cannot read it).

We do not run our own servers and do not use analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.

3. Information saved on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • levels completed, words found, statistics and achievements;
  • in-game coins, daily-challenge streak and chosen themes (coins have no real-money value and cannot be bought);
  • settings such as sound, vibration and theme;
  • the puzzle you are playing, so it can be resumed.

If Android backup is turned on, Android may copy this game data to your Google account backup so it can be restored on a new device. This is managed by Google under your device settings, not by us. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes the data from the device.

4. Advertising

The current version of Word Connect shows no ads. The advertising code in the app is switched off, so no ad is ever requested, Google's consent form is never shown, and Google AdMob receives no information from the game. There are no reward videos.

If we add ads in a future version, they will come from Google AdMob, a member of Google Play's Families Self-Certified Ads SDK program, and will be set up for children: every player treated as a child, ads not personalised, content limited to the G rating, and no advertising ID. We will update this policy before that version is released.

5. Children's privacy

Word Connect is suitable for children and is designed to follow the Google Play Families policy, the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR / UK GDPR rules for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. There is no way in the game to enter or share personal information, the game has no internet access, and the privacy policy and terms open inside the app with no links out.

If you believe a child has somehow sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Permissions

  • Vibration: for optional haptic feedback, which can be turned off in Settings.

7. Your choices and rights

You can turn off sounds and vibration, and delete all game data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage. Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to send, correct or delete on our side, but you are welcome to contact us with any question.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this policy and the date at the top. Important changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
