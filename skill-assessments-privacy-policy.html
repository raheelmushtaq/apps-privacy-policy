Privacy Policy for Skill Assessments

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app Skill Assessments (package com.app.skillassessments, "the app"), published by Hertz Studio ("we", "us"), handles information. If you have any questions, contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com.

Summary

  • There are no accounts, no sign-up and no login. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or location.
  • Quizzes and study notes are built into the app. Your answers and scores are not sent to us.
  • We do not run our own servers and we do not use any analytics or crash-reporting service.
  • The app is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information to show and measure ads, as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The app keeps a small amount of information on your device so it works properly:

  • the quiz and topic content bundled with the app;
  • the answers you choose while a quiz is open, used to show your score and answer review at the end;
  • simple counters used to decide when to show Google Play's in-app review card (for example, when the app was installed and when it last asked).

This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include the app's data in your device backup, which is handled by Google under your Google account settings. You can remove the app's data at any time by clearing its storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Skill Assessments shows banner ads and occasional full-screen ads provided by Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). When ads are loaded, the SDK may collect and process information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate an approximate (city or country level) location, never precise GPS location;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version and language;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped;
  • diagnostic information, such as ad loading performance and SDK errors.

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and, depending on your settings, show personalised ads. This information is collected and used by Google under its own policies, not by us. We do not receive information that identifies you, and we do not sell any data. Data sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • How Google uses advertising data

Tapping an ad may open a web page or another app, such as Google Play. Those are run by the advertiser or by Google under their own privacy policies.

3. Your choices

  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (on most devices: Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads). You can also manage Google ad personalisation at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Deleting data: clear the app's storage or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored on your device.

4. Images and links in quizzes

A few quiz questions include images that are loaded from public image hosts (such as GitHub and Imgur) when you open them. To deliver an image, those hosts receive standard request information such as your IP address and device type, under their own privacy policies. No information about you or your answers is included in these requests.

Some questions and study notes link to external websites, such as official documentation. Those websites open in your browser and are covered by their own privacy policies.

5. Google Play in-app review

From time to time the app may ask Google Play to show its review card. The card is shown and handled by Google Play; we do not see whether you rated the app or what you wrote, other than the public review itself if you post one.

6. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used to load ads and the few online quiz images. Quizzes and study notes work without a connection.
  • Advertising ID: used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK as described above.

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or location permissions.

7. Children's privacy

Skill Assessments is intended for learners, students and job seekers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app or its advertising service, please contact us so we can look into it.

8. Data retention and security

We do not collect or keep any information about you on our side. Information stored by the app stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app. Information collected by Google is kept according to Google's own policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when the app's features change. The updated version will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
