Privacy Policy for Word Search

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Android game Word Search (package com.hertz.wordsearch, the "App"), published by Hertz Studio ("we", "us"), handles information. If you have any questions, contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com.

Summary

  • The App has no accounts, no sign-up and no login.
  • We do not run our own servers and we do not collect, receive or sell your personal information.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Word Search is designed for players of all ages, including children under 13. We treat every player as a child.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob. All ads are non-personalised and rated G (suitable for all ages). The App does not collect the advertising ID, and no interest-based or remarketing ads are shown.
  • Because no personalised advertising takes place, the App does not show an ad consent form.

1. Information stored on your device

The App saves the following on your device so you can keep playing where you left off:

  • Game progress: completed levels and stars per category and difficulty, total puzzles solved, daily puzzle streaks, Beat the Clock best score, hints and reveals you have collected, and the date you last opened the daily reward chest.
  • The puzzle you are currently playing (so you can continue it).
  • Your settings: music, sound effects, vibration and the selected difficulty.

This information never leaves your device through the App and is not sent to us. If Android backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include this game data in your device backup (Google account backup or device-to-device transfer), which is handled by Google under your device's backup settings. You can delete this data at any time by clearing the App's storage (Settings > Apps > Word Search > Storage > Clear storage) or by uninstalling the App.

The App does not request access to your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or precise location.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free to play and is supported by ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). The App shows banner ads, occasional full-screen ads between levels, and optional rewarded video ads that you can choose to watch for a hint, a reveal or extra time.

Google AdMob is certified for use in apps for children under Google Play's Families policy. Every ad request from the App is marked as child-directed (for COPPA) and under the age of consent (for the GDPR and UK GDPR), with a maximum ad content rating of G. This means, for every player:

  • Ads are not personalised, and no interest-based advertising or remarketing is used.
  • The Android advertising ID is not collected (the App removes the advertising ID permission).
  • Only ads suitable for general audiences (rated G) are requested.

To show these contextual ads, limit how often ads appear, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may still process limited information such as:

  • IP address, used to estimate an approximate (city or country level) location, never precise location
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version and language
  • Ad interactions, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as SDK crash logs and ad loading performance

This data is processed by Google under its own policies and under child-directed treatment, not by us. We do not receive information that identifies you personally, and we do not sell any data. Data sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google advertising and AdMob policies: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

Consent and your choices

  • No consent form: the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) only to tell Google that the user is under the age of consent. As a result, no consent form is shown and personalised ads are never requested, wherever you live.
  • Ad privacy choices: the Settings > Ad privacy choices entry only appears if Google's consent tool reports that it is required, which is not expected for this setup.
  • Advertising ID: the app does not read your advertising ID, so resetting it in your device settings has no effect on this app.
  • Rewarded video ads are always optional and clearly labelled.

3. Internet access

The App uses the internet only to load ads and to check the consent status with Google (no consent form is shown). It removes the advertising ID permission that the Google Mobile Ads SDK would otherwise add. Puzzles are generated on your device, so the game itself can be played without a connection.

4. Analytics and crash reporting

The App does not include any separate analytics or crash-reporting service of its own. (The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect diagnostic data as described in section 2.)

5. Children's privacy

Word Search is designed for players of all ages, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We follow Google Play's Families policy and treat every player as a child, whatever their age:

  • We do not ask for or collect any personal information. There is no account, chat, user-generated content or in-app purchase.
  • Every ad request is tagged as child-directed under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and as under the age of consent under the GDPR and UK GDPR (including the rules for children, sometimes called "GDPR-K"). Google therefore shows only non-personalised, G-rated ads.
  • The app does not collect the Android advertising ID, and no interest-based advertising or remarketing is used.
  • No ad consent form is shown, because no personalised advertising or consent-based processing takes place.
  • Rewarded videos are optional and clearly labelled; full-screen ads appear only at natural breaks between games.

Parents and guardians can contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com with any question, or to ask us to delete data. We (the developer) hold no personal data about your child: game progress stays on the device and can be deleted by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App, and we will help you with any request about ad-related data processed by Google.

6. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent or share any personal information, because we do not collect any. Data collected by Google AdMob is handled by Google as described above.

7. Data retention and deletion

We hold no data about you. Data stored on your device stays there until you clear the App's storage or uninstall it. Data processed by Google for child-directed ads is handled under https://policies.google.com/privacy; contact us and we will help with any request.

8. Security

Game data is stored in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot access. Communication between the ads SDK and Google's servers is encrypted (HTTPS).

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if the App adds new features. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed. Significant changes will be reflected on this page.
