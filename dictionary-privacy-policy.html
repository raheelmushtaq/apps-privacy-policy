Privacy Policy for Dictionary

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Android app Dictionary (package com.hertz.dictionary, "the app"), published by Hertz Studio ("we", "us"), handles information. If you have any questions, contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com.

Summary

  • The app has no accounts and no sign-in. It is free and shows ads from Google AdMob.
  • The dictionary itself is stored inside the app and works offline.
  • Your search history, saved words and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Camera images and photos are processed on your device and are never uploaded by the app.
  • The app uses the internet to download translation language packs from Google and to load ads.
  • We, the developer, do not run any server and do not receive any of your data.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps the following data in its private storage on your phone:

  • Search history (up to 200 recently looked-up words, with the time of the lookup)
  • Saved words (words you mark as favourites, with the time they were saved)
  • Settings: the language you translate into, whether to download language packs on Wi-Fi only, whether to translate meanings, theme (system/light/dark), reading speed, and whether you have completed the welcome screens
  • Translation language packs you choose to download (about 30 MB each)

This data never leaves your device through the app. You can clear your search history at any time (Saved → History → Clear history), remove individual saved words, and delete downloaded language packs in Settings. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes all of it.

Device backup: the app allows Android's standard backup. If you have turned on backup for your Google account in your device settings, Android may include the app's settings, history and saved words in your encrypted device backup, which is managed by Google under your Google account, not by us. You can turn backup off in your device settings.

Camera and photos

The app asks for camera permission only when you use the Scan feature, to read printed text so you can tap a word to look it up or translate it. You can also pick a photo with the Android photo picker, which gives the app access only to the photo you choose. Text recognition happens entirely on your device. Images are not saved and are not sent anywhere by the app.

Voice input and reading aloud

  • Voice search / speaking text uses the speech recognition service installed on your device (for example, the one provided by your phone's manufacturer or Google). The app asks the system for offline recognition where available, but the speech service is provided by a third party and is governed by that provider's privacy policy. The app only receives the recognised text.
  • Read aloud uses your device's text-to-speech engine.

Translation and Google ML Kit

Text recognition, language detection and translation are performed on your device by Google ML Kit. The text you translate and the images you scan are processed locally and are not sent to Google or to us.

To translate, the app downloads language models from Google's servers the first time you choose a language. These downloads are encrypted in transit (HTTPS). As part of providing these on-device features, the ML Kit SDK may send limited technical information to Google, such as device model, operating system version, app package name and version, SDK performance metrics and error codes, and a per-installation identifier that does not identify you personally. This information is used by Google to maintain and improve ML Kit. See Google's ML Kit terms and data disclosure: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms and https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google: your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, app interactions (such as ad views and taps), and diagnostic information (such as device model, OS version and crash data). This data is handled by Google under its own policies; see "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services": https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's AdMob data disclosure: https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure

Your choices:

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, or another region where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent (through Google's consent form) before ads use your data, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).
  • Ads are limited to content rated suitable for teens and older.

We, the developer, do not receive any personal data from AdMob; we see only aggregated reports (such as how many ads were shown).

Information we do not collect

We do not ourselves collect your name, email address, contacts, location, advertising ID, payment information or any other personal information. Apart from the Google Mobile Ads SDK described above, the app contains no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Sharing with other apps

When you select text in another app and choose "Define", or share text to Dictionary, the other app sends that text to Dictionary so it can be looked up. Dictionary does not send it anywhere else. If you copy a translation, it is placed on your device's clipboard.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. As a general-purpose English dictionary, it contains definitions of all English words, including some vulgar or offensive words.

Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage on your device, protected by Android's app sandbox. Network downloads of language packs use encrypted connections.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.
