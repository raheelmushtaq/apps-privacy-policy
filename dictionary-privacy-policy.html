Privacy Policy

LinguaScan: Photo Translator by Hertz Studio · Effective 23 November 2026

The short version

  • There are no accounts, and we don't ask for your name, email or location.
  • The dictionary is built into the app and works offline.
  • Your search history, saved words and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Camera images and photos are read on your device and are never uploaded or stored.
  • The app goes online only to download translation language packs from Google.
  • There are no ads in the current version, and we run no server that receives your data.

1. Who we are

LinguaScan: Photo Translator (package com.hertz.dictionary, "the app") is made by Hertz Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

2. Information saved on your device

The app keeps the following only in its private storage on your device:

  • Search history: up to 200 words you looked up recently, with the time of each lookup;
  • Saved words: words you mark as favourites, with the time you saved them;
  • Settings: the language you translate into, whether to translate meanings, whether to download on Wi-Fi only, theme, reading speed and whether you have finished the welcome screens;
  • Translation language packs you choose to download (about 30 MB each);
  • a few counters used to decide when to ask for a rating (see section 7).

You can clear your search history, remove saved words and delete language packs in the app. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your device settings, deletes all of it.

Device backup: if you have turned on Android backup for your Google account, Android may include your settings, history and saved words in your device backup, which Google manages under your account, not us. The dictionary and language packs are not backed up. You can turn backup off in your device settings.

3. Camera and photos

The app uses your camera only while the scanner is open, to read printed text so you can tap a word to look it up or translate it. When you pick a photo, Android's photo picker gives the app only the one image you choose. Text is recognised on your device by Google ML Kit. Images are not saved and are not sent anywhere by the app.

4. Voice input and reading aloud

  • Speaking a word or sentence uses the speech recognition service installed on your device (for example from Google or your phone's maker). The app asks it to work offline where it can, but that service is provided by a third party under its own privacy policy. The app receives only the recognised text.
  • Read aloud uses your device's own text-to-speech engine.

5. Translation and Google ML Kit

Text recognition, language detection and translation run on your device using Google ML Kit. The text you translate and the images you scan are processed locally and are not sent to Google or to us.

The first time you choose a language, the app downloads its translation model from Google's servers over an encrypted connection. To provide these features, ML Kit may send Google limited technical information such as the device model, Android version, app package name and version, performance metrics, error codes and a random per-installation identifier that does not identify you. Google uses it to run and improve ML Kit. See Google's ML Kit terms and ML Kit data disclosure.

6. Text from other apps

When you select text in another app and choose Define, or share text to LinguaScan, that app passes the text to LinguaScan so it can be looked up or translated. LinguaScan does not send it anywhere else. Copy places text on your device's clipboard, and Share sends it to the app you choose, which handles it under its own privacy policy.

7. Ratings and reviews

After you have used the app for a while, it may ask Google Play to show its rating card. The app only counts, on your device, how many words you have looked up, on how many days, and when it last asked, so that it asks rarely. The card is shown and handled entirely by Google Play: any rating or review you give goes to Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy, and we see only what Google Play publishes on the app's public listing.

8. Permissions

  • Camera: to scan text. You can refuse it and still type, speak or pick a photo.
  • Internet and network state: to download translation language packs, and to wait for Wi-Fi when you ask it to.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, microphone, storage or advertising ID.

9. Advertising and analytics

The current version of the app shows no ads and contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting code. If we add ads in a future update, we will update this policy before that update is released, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. As a general English dictionary, it includes definitions of all kinds of words, some of which are vulgar or offensive.

11. Your rights and choices

Your data is under your control on your device: delete it in the app, clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access or delete personal information about you. Because we do not receive your data, we hold nothing about you, but you can contact us with any question.

12. Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Language packs are downloaded over encrypted connections.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top, post the new version at this address and include it in an app update.
