StreakKeeper Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026 App: StreakKeeper (Android, package com.hertz.streakkeeper) Developer: Hertz Studio Contact: raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how the StreakKeeper habit tracker app ("StreakKeeper", "the app", "we", "us") handles information. We built StreakKeeper so that your habit data stays on your device.

1. Summary

  • StreakKeeper does not require an account, sign-up, or login.
  • The habits you create, your check-in history, streak freezes, and app settings are stored only on your device. We do not operate any server and we never receive this data.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects certain device and usage data to serve and measure ads, as described below.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally, in an on-device database and preferences file:

  • Habits: name, emoji icon, color, schedule (every day or specific weekdays), optional reminder time, and creation date.
  • Check-ins: the days you completed each habit and the time you marked it done.
  • Streak freezes: how many freezes you have and which days they were applied to.
  • Settings: theme (System / Light / Dark), reminder preference, and counters used to limit how often ads are shown.

This information is not transmitted to us or to any third party by the app. You can edit or delete any habit at any time inside the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes all of this data from your device.

Android device backup: StreakKeeper allows Android's built-in backup and device-to-device transfer. If you have enabled backup in your device settings, Android may include the app's local data in your device backup (for example, your Google account backup) and restore it when you set up a new device. This backup is managed by Android and your Google account settings, not by us, and we cannot access it. You can turn backup off in your device's system settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

StreakKeeper is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob:

  • a banner ad at the bottom of the Today screen;
  • an occasional full-screen (interstitial) ad after you save a habit;
  • an optional rewarded ad you may choose to watch to earn a streak freeze.

To provide, personalize (where permitted), measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android Advertising ID (the app declares the AD_ID permission for this purpose);
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general (approximate) location;
  • App interaction and ad interaction data, such as ad impressions and clicks;
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash logs and performance data related to the ads SDK;
  • Device information, such as device model, operating system version, and language.

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device). You can also manage ad settings at https://adssettings.google.com.

Data sent to Google by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

4. Notifications and reminders

If you set a reminder for a habit, the app schedules a local notification on your device using Android's alarm service. Reminders are created and delivered entirely on your device; no reminder data is sent anywhere. On Android 13 and later, notifications are shown only if you grant the notification permission (the app asks when you turn on Habit reminders in Settings), and you can turn notifications off at any time in system settings.

5. Permissions

  • Post notifications - to show the habit reminders you choose to set.
  • Run at startup (receive boot completed) - to restore your reminders after the device restarts.
  • Internet and network state - used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to load ads.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) - used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising and measurement.

The app does not request access to your contacts, location (GPS), camera, microphone, photos, files, or accounts.

6. Fonts

The app may request its display fonts (Manrope and Inter) through the Google Play services Downloadable Fonts provider already installed on your device. No habit data or personal information is included in these requests.

7. Children

StreakKeeper is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the ads in this app, contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • Habit data: kept on your device until you delete the habit, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. We hold no copy, so we cannot recover or delete it for you.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google according to Google's policies. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA/UK or under US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not collect or store personal data on our own servers, most requests are best handled by managing or deleting data directly in the app or by contacting Google regarding advertising data. You may still contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com with any privacy question.

10. Security

Your habit data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access on a standard Android device. Data sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with a new effective date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
