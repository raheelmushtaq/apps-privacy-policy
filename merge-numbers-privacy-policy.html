Privacy Policy for Merge Numbers

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Android app Merge Numbers (package com.hertz.mergenumbers, "the app") handles information. The app is published by Hertz Studio ("we", "us").

If you have any questions, contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal details.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device. We don't run servers, and we don't receive or store your game data.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob and Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform) collect some device and usage data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Section 3 explains this.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the UK, EEA and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device using Android's local storage (DataStore):

  • Settings: music, sound effects and vibration on/off
  • Best scores for each board size and for Survival mode
  • Unlocked levels, Daily Challenge progress and your daily streak
  • Number of games played
  • Your power-ups (Shuffle and Hammer) and when you last opened the daily reward chest
  • Your in-progress games, so you can continue where you left off

We never receive this data. It stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.

Android backup: if you have turned on Android's backup, Android may include this game data in your device backup (stored in your Google account) and restore it when you reinstall the app or move to a new phone. Android and Google handle this backup under your Google account settings. We can't access it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show banner ads, interstitial ads (shown at most occasionally between games) and optional rewarded video ads (shown only when you choose to watch one, for example to get an extra undo or a power-up).

When ads are requested, Google may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • Device identifiers: the Android advertising ID, app set ID and similar identifiers
  • Approximate location: derived from your IP address
  • App interactions: for example ad views, ad clicks and app launches
  • Diagnostics and performance data: for example crash logs and ad loading performance related to the SDK
  • Device information: for example device model, operating system version, language and network type

Google uses this information to show ads, personalise ads (only where you have allowed it or where the law permits it), measure ad performance, and detect fraud and abuse. The data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

To learn more about how Google uses this data, see:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google advertising policies and ad personalisation: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Your choices

  • Consent (UK / EEA / Switzerland and other regions where it is required): when you start the app, Google's User Messaging Platform may show a consent form. You can accept or decline personalised advertising. You can change your choice later in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices (this option appears when it applies in your region).
  • US state privacy laws: where required, the consent tool may also give you a way to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (on most devices: Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • If you decline personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised ads, which are based on general context rather than your profile.

4. Permissions

The app requests only these permissions:

  • Internet (INTERNET): to load ads and the consent form.
  • Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): to check whether a connection is available before loading ads.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK): lets the ads SDK read the advertising ID, as described in section 3.

The app does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, precise (GPS) location or phone calls.

5. Data we do not collect

  • We don't collect or store any personal information on our own servers.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services other than what is built into Google's ads SDK.
  • There are no in-app purchases, chat, user accounts or user-generated content.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com and we will help address it.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Game data on your device: you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.
  • Advertising data held by Google: this is governed by Google's privacy policy. You can reset your advertising ID or manage your ad settings as described in section 3. For other requests, see https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Because we don't hold any personal data ourselves, we have nothing to delete on our side. You are still welcome to contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com with any privacy question.

8. Security

Your game data stays on your device and is protected by Android's app sandbox. Data sent by the ads SDK to Google is encrypted in transit.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to object to processing or withdraw consent. Since we don't store personal data ourselves, most of these requests concern Google as the ad provider. You can exercise your consent choices in the app as described above, and contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com if you need help.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We'll post any changes on this page and update the "Last updated" date above.
