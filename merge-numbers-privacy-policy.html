Privacy Policy for Merge Numbers

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Android app Merge Numbers (package com.hertz.mergenumbers, "the app") handles information. The app is published by Hertz Studio ("we", "us").

If you have any questions, contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal details.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device. We don't run servers, and we don't receive or store your game data.
  • Merge Numbers is designed for players of all ages, including children under 13. We treat every player as a child.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. All ads are non-personalised and rated G (suitable for all ages). The app doesn't collect the advertising ID, and no interest-based or remarketing ads are shown. Section 3 explains this.
  • Because no personalised advertising takes place, the app doesn't show an ad consent form.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device using Android's local storage (DataStore):

  • Settings: music, sound effects and vibration on/off
  • Best scores for each board size and for Survival mode
  • Unlocked levels, Daily Challenge progress and your daily streak
  • Number of games played
  • Your power-ups (Shuffle and Hammer) and when you last opened the daily reward chest
  • Your in-progress games, so you can continue where you left off

We never receive this data. It stays on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.

Android backup: if you have turned on Android's backup, Android may include this game data in your device backup (stored in your Google account) and restore it when you reinstall the app or move to a new phone. Android and Google handle this backup under your Google account settings. We can't access it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show banner ads, interstitial ads (shown at most occasionally between games) and optional rewarded video ads (shown only when you choose to watch one, for example to get an extra undo or a power-up).

Google AdMob is certified for use in apps for children under Google Play's Families policy. Every ad request from the app is marked as child-directed (for COPPA) and under the age of consent (for the GDPR and UK GDPR), with a maximum ad content rating of G. This means, for every player:

  • Ads are not personalised, and no interest-based advertising or remarketing is used.
  • The Android advertising ID is not collected (the app removes the advertising ID permission).
  • Only ads suitable for general audiences (rated G) are requested.

To show these contextual ads, limit how often ads appear, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may still process limited information such as:

  • IP address, used to estimate an approximate (city or country level) location, never precise location
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version and language
  • Ad interactions, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as SDK crash logs and ad loading performance

This data is processed by Google under its own policies and under child-directed treatment, not by us. We do not receive information that identifies you personally, and we do not sell any data. Data sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

To learn more about how Google uses this data, see:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google advertising policies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Your choices

  • No consent form: the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) only to tell Google that the user is under the age of consent. As a result, no consent form is shown and personalised ads are never requested, wherever you live.
  • Ad privacy choices: the Settings → Ad privacy choices entry only appears if Google's consent tool reports that it is required, which is not expected for this setup.
  • Advertising ID: the app does not read your advertising ID, so resetting it in your device settings has no effect on this app.
  • Rewarded video ads are always optional and clearly labelled.

4. Permissions

The app requests only these permissions:

  • Internet (INTERNET): to load ads and check the consent status with Google.
  • Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): to check whether a connection is available before loading ads.

The app removes the Advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) that the Google Mobile Ads SDK would otherwise add, so the advertising ID can't be read.

The app does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, precise (GPS) location or phone calls.

5. Data we do not collect

  • We don't collect or store any personal information on our own servers.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services other than what is built into Google's ads SDK.
  • There are no in-app purchases, chat, user accounts or user-generated content.

6. Children

Merge Numbers is designed for players of all ages, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We follow Google Play's Families policy and treat every player as a child, whatever their age:

  • We do not ask for or collect any personal information. There is no account, chat, user-generated content or in-app purchase.
  • Every ad request is tagged as child-directed under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and as under the age of consent under the GDPR and UK GDPR (including the rules for children, sometimes called "GDPR-K"). Google therefore shows only non-personalised, G-rated ads.
  • The app does not collect the Android advertising ID, and no interest-based advertising or remarketing is used.
  • No ad consent form is shown, because no personalised advertising or consent-based processing takes place.
  • Rewarded videos are optional and clearly labelled; full-screen ads appear only at natural breaks between games.

Parents and guardians can contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com with any question, or to ask us to delete data. We (the developer) hold no personal data about your child: game progress stays on the device and can be deleted by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app, and we will help you with any request about ad-related data processed by Google.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Game data on your device: you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.
  • Advertising data held by Google: this is governed by Google's privacy policy and processed under child-directed treatment (section 3). For requests, see https://policies.google.com/privacy, or contact us and we'll help.

Because we don't hold any personal data ourselves, we have nothing to delete on our side. You are still welcome to contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com with any privacy question.

8. Security

Your game data stays on your device and is protected by Android's app sandbox. Data sent by the ads SDK to Google is encrypted in transit.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to object to processing or withdraw consent. Since we don't store personal data ourselves, most of these requests concern Google as the ad provider. Because every player is treated as a child, no consent-based or personalised advertising takes place. Parents and guardians can contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com if they need help.
