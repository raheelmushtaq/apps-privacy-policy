Privacy Policy for Orb Blast: Ball Shooter

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android game Orb Blast: Ball Shooter (package com.hertz.orbblast, "the app"), published by Hertz Studio (developer: Raheel Mushtaq, "we", "us"), handles information. If you have questions, contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com.

Summary

  • The app has no accounts, no sign-up and no login, and no in-app purchases.
  • Your progress, items and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run any servers and we do not receive this data.
  • Orb Blast is designed for players of all ages, including children under 13. We treat every player as a child.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. All ads are non-personalized and rated G (suitable for all ages). The app does not collect the advertising ID, and no interest-based or remarketing ads are shown.
  • If the app crashes, an anonymous crash report is sent to Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the problem. It contains no name, email or other personal details.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device, using Android's local app storage:

  • Game progress: unlocked levels and the stars and best score for each, Endless and Boss mode records, Daily Challenge results and weekly mission progress
  • Coins, cannon upgrades, weapons and cannon skins
  • Achievements, your daily reward streak, game statistics (such as orbs destroyed and play time) and a history of your last 50 games
  • Whether you have finished the tutorial and which orb types you have already seen
  • Settings: sound effects, vibration and screen shake

This data never leaves your device through the app and is not sent to us. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this data in your device's backup to your Google account, or copy it to a new device during setup. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us. You can delete all of this data at any time by clearing the app's storage (Settings > Apps > Orb Blast > Storage > Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free to play and is supported by ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). The app shows banner ads on menu screens (never during gameplay), occasional full-screen ads at natural breaks between levels (at most one every three levels and two minutes apart), and optional rewarded video ads that you can choose to watch for in-game rewards: a revive, doubled coins after a level, a doubled daily reward or free coins.

Google AdMob is certified for use in apps for children under Google Play's Families policy. Every ad request from the app is marked as child-directed (for COPPA) and under the age of consent (for the GDPR and UK GDPR), with a maximum ad content rating of G. This means, for every player:

  • Ads are not personalized, and no interest-based advertising or remarketing is used.
  • The Android advertising ID is not collected (the app removes the advertising ID permissions).
  • Only ads suitable for general audiences (rated G) are requested.

To show these contextual ads, limit how often ads appear, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may still process limited information such as:

  • IP address, used to estimate an approximate (city or country level) location, never precise location
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, app version and language
  • Ad interactions, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as SDK crash logs and ad loading performance

This data is processed by Google under its own policies and under child-directed treatment, not by us. We do not receive information that identifies you personally, and we do not sell any data. Data sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Google uses this information as described in its policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google advertising technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

3. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes, it sends a crash report to Google's Firebase Crashlytics so we can find and fix the bug. A crash report contains:

  • Technical details of the crash (the stack trace and the app state at the time)
  • Device information, such as device model, operating system version, app version and free memory
  • A random Crashlytics installation identifier, used only to count how many devices are affected. It is not the advertising ID and is not linked to you.

Crash reports do not include your name, email, contacts, location or any game content you could identify yourself by. They are used only to fix problems, are not used for advertising, are not shared with anyone else, and are handled by Google under the Firebase terms (https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy). Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days.

4. Consent and your choices

  • No consent form: the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) only to tell Google that the user is under the age of consent. As a result, no consent form is shown and personalized ads are never requested, wherever you live.
  • Privacy choices: the Settings > Privacy choices button only appears if Google's consent tool reports that it is required, which is not expected for this setup.
  • Advertising ID: the app does not read your advertising ID, so resetting it in your device settings has no effect on this app.
  • Rewarded video ads are always optional and clearly labelled.

5. Internet and permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only to load and show ads, to check the consent status with Google and to send crash reports.
  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback in the game. You can turn it off in Settings.

The app removes the advertising ID permissions that the Google Mobile Ads SDK would otherwise add. It does not ask for the location, camera, microphone, contacts, notifications or storage permissions. The game can be played fully offline (ads are not shown while offline).

6. Data we collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store, sell or receive any personal information from the app. Apart from the anonymous crash reports described above, we do not use analytics. Data processed by Google AdMob and Firebase Crashlytics is handled by Google as described above.

7. Data security

Information sent to Google's ad and crash-reporting services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). Data stored on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox.

8. Children

Orb Blast is designed for players of all ages, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We follow Google Play's Families policy and treat every player as a child, whatever their age:

  • We do not ask for or collect any personal information. There is no account, chat, user-generated content or in-app purchase.
  • Every ad request is tagged as child-directed under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and as under the age of consent under the GDPR and UK GDPR. Google therefore shows only non-personalized, G-rated ads.
  • The app does not collect the Android advertising ID, and no interest-based advertising or remarketing is used.
  • Rewarded videos are optional and clearly labelled; full-screen ads appear only between levels, never during gameplay.

Parents and guardians can contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com with any question, or to ask us to delete data. We hold no personal data about your child: game progress stays on the device and can be deleted by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app, and we will help with any request about data processed by Google.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to or restrict its use. Because we do not hold any of your data, you can exercise these rights over the data on your device by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it, and over advertising or crash data processed by Google by contacting Google or asking us for help at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com.
