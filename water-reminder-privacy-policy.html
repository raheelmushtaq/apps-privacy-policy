Privacy Policy for Hydra Water Reminder & Tracker

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This policy explains what information the Hydra Water Reminder & Tracker app ("Hydra", "the app", package com.hertz.waterreminder), published by Hertz Studio ("we", "us"), handles, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • Hydra has no account, no sign-up and no cloud sync. We do not operate a server that receives your data.
  • Your profile and water log are stored only on your device.
  • The only data that leaves your device through the app is collected by Google AdMob, the advertising service that supports the free app.
  • Hydra does not include any analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own.

Information stored on your device

To work, Hydra stores the following locally (in an on-device database and preferences file):

  • Profile you enter during setup: name (optional), gender, age, weight, height, activity level, climate, wake-up and bedtime, preferred unit and daily water goal.
  • Hydration log: each drink amount and time, daily totals and goal progress.
  • App settings: reminder on/off and interval, unit, theme, color preference, and internal progress counters.

This information is used only inside the app to calculate your suggested goal, show your progress, history and statistics, and decide when to send reminders. We never receive, sell or share it.

Android backup

If you have Android's built-in backup turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's on-device data in your device backup and restore it when you reinstall or move to a new phone. This backup is performed by Android and stored in your Google account under Google's terms; we have no access to it. You can manage it in your device's Settings → Google → Backup (wording varies by device).

Information collected by advertising (Google AdMob)

Hydra shows a banner ad and occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads through Google AdMob. Google's Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform SDK may automatically collect and process:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID and app set ID)
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (approximate, not precise)
  • Ad interactions (for example, impressions and taps) and app usage needed to serve ads
  • Diagnostic and performance information about the SDK
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language)

This data is used to serve, measure and personalize ads, to prevent fraud and abuse, and is processed by Google under its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data sent to Google is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Your ad choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where consent is required, Hydra uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before ads are requested, and respects your choice.
  • On Android you can reset your Advertising ID or delete it / opt out of ads personalization in Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on device).
  • You can uninstall the app at any time to stop all collection by the advertising SDK.

Hydra does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos/files, or health-platform (Health Connect) access.

Health information

Weight, height, age and hydration logs are personal wellness information. They stay on your device, are never transmitted by us, and are never used for advertising. Hydra is a general wellness app and is not a medical device; it does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.

Children's privacy

Hydra is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are not configured for child-directed treatment.

Data retention and deletion

Because your profile and hydration data stay on your device, you control them directly:

  • Settings → Data → Reset all data deletes all drinks, history and app settings.
  • To also remove your profile, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → Hydra → Storage → Clear storage. Uninstalling removes all locally stored data.
  • Advertising data held by Google can be managed through your device's ad settings and Google's privacy controls described above. Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; you can still contact us with any question.

Security

On-device data is protected by Android's app sandbox. We do not transmit your profile or water log anywhere.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date above will be updated, and material changes will be noted in the app's store listing.
