Privacy Policy for Block Puzzle

Effective date: October 18, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Android app Block Puzzle (package com.hertz.blockpuzzle, "the app"), published by Hertz Studio ("we", "us"), handles information. If you have questions, contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com.

Summary

  • You do not need an account. The app has no sign-up or login.
  • We (the developer) do not run any server and do not receive, store or sell your personal information.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect certain device data to show ads, as described below.
  • Users in the UK, EEA, Switzerland and certain US states are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and can change their choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following information locally on your device, using Android's app storage (DataStore):

  • Settings: music, sound effects and vibration on/off
  • Best scores (Classic and Survival)
  • Your in-progress games (board, pieces, score, combo) so you can continue later
  • Level progress, Daily Challenge progress and day streak
  • Booster counts and the date you last opened the daily reward chest
  • A count of games played (kept only on your device)

This information never leaves your device through us. It is not linked to your identity.

Android backup: if you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this game data in your device backup (for example to your Google account) so your progress can be restored after reinstalling or moving to a new device. This backup is handled by Google/Android under your device settings, not by us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is supported by ads: banner ads, occasional full-screen (interstitial) ads between games, and optional rewarded videos you can choose to watch for a continue or extra boosters.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To deliver, measure and personalize ads (where you allow it), and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • Device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Device and app information (device model, operating system version, app version, language)
  • Interactions with ads (for example ad views and taps) and app usage information
  • Diagnostic and performance information (for example SDK crash logs and ad load times)

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies. We do not receive information that identifies you personally. Learn more:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Ad settings: https://adssettings.google.com

Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit.

Consent and your choices

  • Consent (GDPR / UK GDPR / US state laws): the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). When required in your region, a consent form is shown when the app starts, before ads are requested. You can choose whether to allow personalized ads.
  • Change your choice: where required, open Settings > Ad privacy choices in the app to review or change your consent at any time.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

3. Permissions

The app requests only:

  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET): to load ads.
  • Network state (android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): used by the ads SDK to check connectivity before loading ads.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to read the advertising ID for ads.

The app does not request access to your location (GPS), contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

4. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, files or payment information. The app has no in-app purchases, chat, or user-generated content.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Game data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage (Android Settings > Apps > Block Puzzle > Storage > Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app.
  • Because we do not hold any of your data on our own servers, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. For data processed by Google for advertising, you can reset your advertising ID in device settings or use Google's tools at https://myaccount.google.com.

6. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us at raheelmushtaq41@gmail.com and we will help address it.

7. Security

Game data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access. Network communication by the ads SDK uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

We may update this policy when the app changes. The updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
