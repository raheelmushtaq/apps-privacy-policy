Privacy Policy

QR Barcode Scanner & Generator by Hertz Studio · Effective 21 November 2026

The short version

  • Codes are read on your device. Camera frames and gallery images are never uploaded or stored.
  • Your scan and create history is saved only on your device, and you can delete it at any time.
  • There are no accounts and we don't ask for your name, email or location.
  • When the app crashes, an anonymous crash report is sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix it.
  • There are no ads in the current version.

1. Who we are

QR Barcode Scanner & Generator ("the app") is made by Hertz Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have.

2. Camera and scanning

The app uses your camera only while the scanner is open, to find QR codes and barcodes. Each frame is analysed on your device by Google ML Kit and then discarded. We never record, save or upload camera images.

When you choose Scan from Gallery, Android's picker gives the app only the one image you select. It is read on your device to find a code and is not copied or uploaded.

3. Information saved on your device

To show your history, the app saves the following only in its private storage on your device:

  • the content of codes you scan or create (for example a link, text, phone number, contact card, calendar event or Wi-Fi name and password);
  • the type of code, an optional title and the date;
  • the image of codes you create, including any logo picture you choose to add to a code.

You can delete any item from History or from its detail page. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your device settings, deletes everything. The app's data is not included in Android backups.

4. Information sent to Google

The app uses two Google services. Neither receives your codes, your history or your images.

  • Firebase Crashlytics: if the app crashes, it sends a crash report so we can find and fix the problem. A report contains the error details (stack trace), the app version, the device model, Android version, available memory and disk space, the state of the app at the time (for example screen orientation) and random installation identifiers that count how many installs are affected. These identifiers do not reveal who you are. Google keeps crash data for 90 days.
  • Google ML Kit (the code reader): may send Google anonymous diagnostics such as the device model, Android version, app version, how fast scanning runs and error codes, together with a random per-installation identifier. It does not send images or what a code contains.

This data is encrypted in transit and is processed by Google on our behalf, under the Firebase privacy and security terms and the Google Privacy Policy. It may be processed on Google servers outside your country. We do not sell any data, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Actions that open other apps

When you tap an action such as Open, Call, Send SMS, Send email, Show on map, Add to contacts, Add to calendar, Share or Wi-Fi settings, the app passes the code's content to the app you choose (for example your browser, dialer or contacts app). That app handles the information under its own privacy policy. Copy places the content on your device's clipboard.

Links in a scanned code can lead to any website. The app shows you the full content first; only open links you trust.

6. Permissions

  • Camera: to scan codes. You can refuse it and still scan from your gallery or create codes.
  • Internet and network state: to send crash reports and ML Kit diagnostics.

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, calendar, microphone or storage. Adding a contact or an event opens your own contacts or calendar app, where you decide whether to save it.

7. Advertising

The current version of the app shows no ads and contains no advertising code. If we add ads in a future update, we will update this policy before that update is released, and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights and choices

Your history is under your control on your device: delete items in the app, clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access or delete personal information about you. Because crash reports are not linked to your identity we usually cannot single out yours, but you can contact us and we will help as far as we can.

10. Security

Your history stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Crash reports and diagnostics are sent over encrypted connections.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and include the new version in an app update.
