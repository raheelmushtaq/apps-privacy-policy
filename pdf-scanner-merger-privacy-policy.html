Privacy Policy - PDF Scanner & Merger

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the Android app PDF Scanner & Merger (package com.hertz.pdfscanner, the "App"), published by Hertz Studio ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • The App does not require an account or login.
  • Your documents, scans, photos and PDFs are processed on your device. We do not upload them to any server and we cannot see them.
  • The App shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID) and related data to deliver and measure ads.
  • The App uses Google's ML Kit Document Scanner (part of Google Play services), which runs on your device. Google may collect limited diagnostic and usage information about the scanner component.
  • We (the developer) do not operate any server and do not collect, store or sell your personal data.

3. Permissions

  • Camera - used only to scan documents with the built-in scanner. It is requested when you open the scanner and you can deny or revoke it at any time in Android settings. Other features keep working without it.
  • Internet and network state - used to load ads and by Google Play services components. The PDF tools themselves do not need an internet connection.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID) and Android ad services permissions - used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising and ad measurement.

The App does not request access to your contacts, location (GPS), microphone, or all files on your storage.

4. Third-party services

Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)

The App displays banner and interstitial ads. To serve, personalize (where permitted), measure ads and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share with Google:

  • Device or other identifiers, such as the advertising ID and app set ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • App interactions with ads, and app information and performance data (for example, crash and diagnostic data)

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services - https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites Google Privacy Policy - https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google ML Kit Document Scanner (Google Play services)

Document detection and scanning run on your device. Scanned images are returned to the App and are not sent to Google by the App. Google Play services may download the scanner models and may collect limited diagnostic and usage information (such as device model, OS version, app package name and version, and performance metrics) to maintain and improve the service. Learn more: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure

5. Your choices

  • Advertising ID: You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).
  • Camera: You can revoke camera permission at any time in Android settings.
  • Delete your data: Delete individual documents inside the App, or clear the App's storage or uninstall it to remove all documents, the document list and settings from your device. Because we do not hold your data on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Data collected by Google is governed by Google's privacy policy and controls.

6. Data security

Your documents remain in the App's private storage on your device. Data sent by Google's SDKs to Google is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.

7. Children's privacy

The App is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
